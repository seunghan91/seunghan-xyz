In Albania, 17% of sampled conversations were matched to software development tasks. In Korea that figure is 10.42%.

## What the data says about Albania

Albania's overall Anthropic Usage Index is 1.02 — rank 59 of 121, essentially the global midpoint, and far below Korea's 3.78. Yet look inside the conversations and the ordering flips: 17% of sampled Albanian conversations matched software development tasks, against 11.51% globally and 10.42% in Korea. That is a 47.7% premium over the world average and a 63.1% lead over the country with nearly four times the overall usage intensity. Anyone assuming a simple rich-country/poor-country ranking of who builds with these tools has the table backwards on this row.

What that plausibly reflects is intent. Content creation and copywriting leads the Albanian topic list at 23.67%, almost exactly the global 22.72%, and the automation-versus-augmentation split — 48.41% to 51.59% — mirrors the global 48.62% nearly to the decimal. Nothing there is unusual. The distinctiveness is concentrated in one place: when conversations in Albania turn technical, they turn to building software at a rate the global average does not match. Meanwhile hobbies and lifestyle conversations run at 7.18%, a quarter below the global 9.49%, which is consistent with usage weighted toward output rather than pastime. That is a reading of the numbers, not a claim about who is typing — the data describes tasks, not people.

The learning figures sharpen the same edge. Conversations matched to coursework or learning tasks make up 20.97% in Albania versus 16.45% globally, and education and learning as a topic sits at 13.92% — 24.2% above Korea's 11.21%. Work conversations hold at 42.01%, close to the global 43.36%. Put together, the Albanian profile is not a lighter version of someone else's usage; it is a specific weighting — study-adjacent and build-heavy — that happens to outscore Korea on software development, education, and even hobbies all at once. One snapshot says nothing about where this is heading, but it says plenty about where the weight already sits.

That profile maps directly onto what Seunghan helps with. Conversations heavy in software development tasks tend to run into the same walls: shipping an app through App Store or Play Store review without burning weeks on rejections, standing up AI agent and MCP tooling that behaves in production, backend and auth decisions that are expensive to redo, mobile development across platforms, and the reliability engineering that keeps something alive after launch.
