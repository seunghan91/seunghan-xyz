## What the data says about Moldova

A small landlocked country between Romania and Ukraine outscores Korea on software. That is the headline in Moldova's data: 14.24% of sampled conversations were matched to software development tasks, versus 10.42% in Korea — a 36.7% lead over one of the most wired economies on earth. Against the global average of 11.51%, Moldova's figure is the single most distinctive number in its whole profile, running +23.7% above it. The country's overall Anthropic Usage Index is 1.45, placing it 46th of 121, well below Korea's 3.78 — so the volume of use is modest, but its shape is not. When people in Moldova open an AI assistant, an unusually large slice of what follows looks like building software.

The surrounding numbers sketch how that building happens. Work-matched conversations make up only 36.7% of the sample, against a global 43.36%, while coursework-and-learning conversations reach 25.14% — nearly nine points above the global 16.45%. Education & Learning as a topic claims 14.29%, again ahead of Korea's 11.21%. One plausible reading, offered as a guess rather than a conclusion: a good share of these sessions are people teaching themselves to build, or learning while they build, rather than executing assigned tasks. The data describes the texture of the conversations, nothing about who is having them.

Two smaller figures round out the picture. The automation-augmentation split sits at 50.77% to 49.23% — almost an even coin flip, with a slight lean toward handing the model whole tasks rather than iterating alongside it. And Hobbies & Lifestyle takes 10.14% of conversations, 57.5% above Korea's 6.44%, the widest Korea-relative gap in the table. Content Creation & Copywriting tops the topic list at 24.76%, close to the global 22.72%, with Research & Intelligence at 9.68% just under the global 10.94%.
