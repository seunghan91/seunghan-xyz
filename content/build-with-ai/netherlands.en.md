In Netherlands, 12.58% of sampled conversations were matched to hobbies & lifestyle tasks. In Korea that figure is 6.44%.

## What the data says about Netherlands

Start with the number that breaks the expected script: 12.58%. That is the share of AI conversations in Netherlands about hobbies and lifestyle — nearly double Korea's 6.44%, a 95.3% lead, and 32.6% above the global average of 9.49%. The surprise compounds when you look at raw intensity: Netherlands carries an Anthropic Usage Index of 3.9, ranked 11th of 121 countries, while Korea — Seunghan's home market and a country many would assume tops any AI-engagement table — records 3.78. So much for the tidy ordering in which a wealthy East Asian tech hub sets the pace and everyone else follows. On this snapshot, Netherlands is not following anyone.

What sits underneath that hobbies figure makes it more interesting still. Work conversations are 40.82% in Netherlands, under the global 43.36%. Coursework-style conversations are 14.64%, under the global 16.45%, and education and learning as a topic runs 9.3% against 13.23% globally. When work and study both under-index and leisure over-indexes, one cautious reading — a guess, but grounded in the gaps — is that these tools have spread into the unpaid, ungraded corners of life: the side project, the weekend experiment, the thing built for its own sake.

The mode of use tells a consistent story. Only 45.27% of conversations lean toward automation, against a global 48.62%; the larger share, 54.73%, leans toward augmentation, treating the model as a working partner rather than a substitute. Conversations matched to sales and related tasks punch in at 11.09% versus a global 9.14%, while conversations matched to software development tasks sit at 9.59%, below the global 11.51% — with content creation (22.51%) and research and intelligence (12.49%) rounding out the top topics.

Which means the constraint for a builder in Netherlands is unlikely to be appetite. It is the engineering layer: shipping apps to stores, AI agent and MCP tooling, backend and auth, mobile development, reliability engineering. That is precisely the set Seunghan works through with people, free and informally, from Seoul — a city whose home-country numbers, on this data, trail yours.
