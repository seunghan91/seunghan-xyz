In Ecuador, 24.73% of sampled conversations were matched to education & learning tasks. In Korea that figure is 11.21%.

## What the data says about Ecuador

The most interesting number in Ecuador's data is 38.64%. That is the share of conversations that look like coursework or learning — and the global average for the same measure is 16.45%. Ecuador's Anthropic Usage Index sits at 0.58, ranked 80 of 121, next to Korea's 3.78. A simple rich-country/poor-country ordering would predict a thin, work-only slice of usage at that index level. Instead, work conversations are 38.63% (below the global 43.36%) while learning-matched conversations run at essentially the same volume, more than double the world norm. The low index describes how much is happening overall, not what kind of thing is happening — and what is happening skews toward understanding, not just output.

The topic mix says the same thing from another angle. Education & Learning accounts for 24.73% of sampled conversations against a global 13.23%, and Content Creation & Copywriting leads at 26.89%. Conversations matched to educational instruction and library job tasks run at 15.69%, above the global 12.79%, while Research & Intelligence sits at 6.36%, well under the global 10.94%. A plausible reading — a guess, but grounded in the split — is that these tools are being used as an explanation layer: drafting and learning intertwined, less of the analyst-style querying that dominates elsewhere. The automation-versus-augmentation balance backs this up: 49.38% automation-leaning, 50.62% augmentation, nearly even and close to the global 48.62%. Nothing about the pattern suggests passive delegation.

Here is where the usual hierarchy inverts. Korea is one of the most saturated AI markets in the sample, yet Ecuador leads it in two categories: Education & Learning, 24.73% to Korea's 11.21% — a 120.6% higher share — and Software Development, 10.5% to Korea's 10.42%. On conversations matched to building software, Ecuador is ahead of Seoul's home market, however slightly. That is not a footnote; it is the headline the index ranking would never tell you.

For a builder in Ecuador, the distance to Seoul is a dozen time zones and none at all — the bottlenecks Seunghan helps with are the same ones these conversations already circle: getting an app through store review and shipped, wiring AI agents and MCP tooling into a real product, standing up backend and auth that holds, making mobile builds behave, and keeping systems reliable once real people depend on them. The data says the appetite to learn and build is plainly there. The offer is a working engineer on the other end of a message, free and informal, glad to look at your code.
