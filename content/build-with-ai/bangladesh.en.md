## What the data says about Bangladesh

The most interesting number in Bangladesh's data is 16.69. That is the share of sampled conversations matched to software development tasks — 60.2% higher than Korea's 10.42% and 45% above the global average of 11.51%. Anyone assuming a simple ordering where the country with the higher usage index does more of everything would expect Korea, at 3.78 against Bangladesh's 0.11, to lead on every serious category. It does not. On conversations about building software, Bangladesh's mix runs well ahead, and that gap is the single most surprising thing in this snapshot.

The lead is wider still on Education & Learning: 22.9% of conversations, more than double Korea's 11.21% and 73.1% above the global 13.23%. Related signals point the same way — 27.07% of conversations looked like coursework or learning against a global 16.45%, and 16.12% matched Educational Instruction and Library job tasks, 26% above global. To be careful about what this means: these are patterns in conversation content, not facts about who is typing. A plausible reading is that people in Bangladesh lean on these tools heavily as teaching and learning instruments — explaining concepts, working through material, building understanding — alongside making things. That is a guess from the shape of the data, but it fits every related figure.

Just as telling is what the mix leaves out. Hobbies & Lifestyle accounts for only 5.68% of conversations, 40% below the global 9.49%, and Office and Administrative Support tasks come in at 6.59% against 7.89%. Work conversations sit at 36.45%, below the global 43.36%, while the automation-versus-augmentation split is nearly even at 50.75% to 49.25%, slightly more automation-leaning than the global 48.62%. Content Creation & Copywriting is the top topic at 24.65%, close to the global 22.72%. Taken together, the profile is concentrated: conversations about learning, writing, and building software, with relatively little spillover into casual use.

None of this is a problem to be solved from abroad — on the categories that matter most for builders, this data has Bangladesh ahead of Korea, plainly.
