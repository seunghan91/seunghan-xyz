## What the data says about Spain

A country at 2.69 on the Anthropic Usage Index — rank 24 of 121 — would not obviously be expected to outscore a country at 3.78 on anything. Yet Spain's sample beats Korea's in three of the five top topic categories. Conversations matched to hobbies and lifestyle tasks account for 9.28% of Spanish conversations against Korea's 6.44%, a 44% gap. Education and learning runs 14.7% versus 11.21%, up 31%. Software development edges it too, 11.34% to 10.42%. Raw usage intensity and breadth of use are different things, and on breadth Spain's numbers hold their own against one of the heaviest AI-using countries measured.

The number I keep coming back to is 19.22%: the share of conversations that look like coursework or learning, against a global average of 16.45%. This describes the conversations, not the people in them — the data cannot tell us who is typing, only what the exchanges resemble. Read alongside the education topic share, it suggests Spanish-language AI sessions skew toward explanation and skill-building rather than pure task execution. That is a guess, but it fits the texture of the rest of the table, where work conversations at 42.28% sit just under the global 43.36%, leaving more room for everything else.

On the automation question Spain lands almost exactly at the global midpoint: 48.1% of conversations lean toward handing tasks over, 51.9% toward working with the tool, against a global automation figure of 48.62%. Content creation and copywriting tops the topic list at 22.06%, research and intelligence holds 10.52%. Nothing here is lopsided. The profile is that of a place using AI across the full width of what it can do, in both Spanish and the country's other languages, without any single use case crowding the others out.

For a reader building something in Spain, none of this is a permission slip — you are already part of these numbers. What I offer is the practical tail of the work: getting an app through App Store and Play review without the rejection spiral, wiring AI agents and MCP tooling so they behave under real load, backend and auth that you stop thinking about, mobile development when the web view is not enough, and reliability engineering for the day your side project gets traffic. Seoul runs hours ahead of mainland Spain, which works in your favour — a question sent in your evening is usually answered before your morning coffee. Free, informal, one builder to another.
