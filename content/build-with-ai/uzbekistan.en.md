## What the data says about Uzbekistan

Uzbekistan's most telling number is one where it beats nearly everyone: 32.67% of sampled AI conversations look like coursework or learning, almost exactly double the global share of 16.45%. The same signal shows up from every angle the data offers. Conversations matched to education and learning topics account for 26.86%, against a global 13.23% — the country's single largest divergence from the world average at +103%. And conversations matched to educational instruction and library job tasks run at 20.42%, versus 12.79% globally. One important caveat: these figures describe what the conversations resembled, not who was having them. A conversation that looks like coursework doesn't tell you anything about the person typing it. But when three independent cuts of the same data point the same direction, the pattern itself is real — in Uzbekistan, these tools are being used heavily as instruments for learning.

Set that against the raw volume figure and the picture gets interesting. Uzbekistan's Anthropic Usage Index is 0.3, ranked 102 of 121 countries, while Korea sits at 3.78. Yet on education and learning conversations, Uzbekistan runs at 26.86% against Korea's 11.21% — 139.6% higher. It also edges Korea on conversations matched to software development tasks, 11.3% to 10.42%, and on hobbies and lifestyle, 8.25% to 6.44%. Lower volume, different composition. Only 22.81% of conversations are work-related, barely half the global 43.36%, and research and intelligence topics come in at 7.4% versus a global 10.94%. A plausible reading, offered as a guess: where usage is more selective, it concentrates on the highest-leverage activity — building capability rather than delegating routine tasks.

On style of use, conversations lean slightly toward automation over augmentation, 53.38% to 46.62%, a bit above the global automation share of 48.62%. Office and administrative support tasks sit below the global average, 6.47% versus 7.89%, consistent with a profile tilted away from routine paperwork and toward making things.
