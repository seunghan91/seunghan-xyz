In Thailand, 15.69% of sampled conversations were matched to software development tasks. In Korea that figure is 10.42%.

## What the data says about Thailand

If you assumed AI usage would order itself neatly by national income, Thailand's data breaks that assumption in the first row. Conversations matched to software development tasks make up 15.69% of Thailand's sampled traffic — against a global average of 11.51%, and against Korea's 10.42%. That is a 50.6% edge over the country I live in, and I write code for a living here. The pattern repeats at the job-task level: conversations matched to computer and mathematical work sit at 28.51%, versus 23.8% globally, the widest positive gap in the dataset. Whatever you expected the ordering to be, the engineering share in Thailand's conversations is the number to sit with.

A plausible reading, and it is a guess, is that these tools arrive in Thai conversations as working equipment rather than as something to study. Work-related conversations account for 53.12% of the sample, nearly ten points above the global 43.36%. Coursework-shaped conversations run at 9.84%, well under the global 16.45%, and education-and-learning topics are about 27% below the world average. The tilt toward automation over augmentation — 52.54% to 47.46%, where globally automation stays under half — fits the same shape: hand the tool a concrete task, take the output, keep moving. That says nothing about who is typing, only about what the conversations look like.

The lead over Korea is not only in code. Hobbies and lifestyle conversations run at 8.67% versus Korea's 6.44%, and content creation tops the topic list at 20.15%, close to the global share. So the profile is broad, with a heavy technical center of gravity. Thailand's overall usage index is 0.73, ranked 69 of 121, far below Korea's 3.78 — but that measures volume per capita, not intensity. The conversations that exist lean hard toward building things.

That is exactly the territory I can be useful in, free and informally. Shipping apps to the App Store or Google Play — certificates, review rejections, the checklist you only learn by failing it — I have done and can shortcut for you. AI agent and MCP tooling is my daily work, so if you are wiring tools into a model, bring the mess. Backend and auth, mobile development, and the reliability work of keeping a live thing alive are all on the table. Bangkok is two hours behind Seoul; your evening is my late evening, and late evening is when I am at my desk anyway.
