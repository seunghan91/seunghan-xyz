In Benin, 18.42% of sampled conversations were matched to education & learning tasks. In Korea that figure is 11.21%.

## What the data says about Benin

Start with the number that breaks the expected ordering: 34.25% of sampled conversations from Benin are matched to content creation and copywriting tasks, against a global average of 22.72% — a 50.7% premium — and ahead of Korea's 27.3%. Whoever assumes that heavier use of AI for making things belongs to richer countries has the picture backwards here. Benin's overall usage index is 0.39, far below Korea's 3.78, but the index measures volume; composition tells a different story, and on composition Benin leans harder into production than either the world average or the country I am writing from.

The learning texture is just as pronounced. Conversations resembling coursework make up 27.03% of Benin's total, well above the global 16.45%, and education and learning topics take an 18.42% share against 13.23% worldwide — 39.2% above baseline. One caution: these are labels on conversations, not descriptions of the people having them; a conversation that looks like coursework tells us nothing about who typed it. Still, the combination is suggestive — a plausible, hedged reading is that many sessions in Benin move fluidly between learning a thing and making a thing, sometimes within the same conversation.

Software work fits the same pattern. Conversations matched to software development tasks hold 14.89% of the sample, above the global 11.51% and 42.9% above Korea's 10.42%. Add job-task matches for arts, design, entertainment, sports, and media at 17.54% (global 13.55%) and the outline sharpens: Benin's conversations cluster around building and creating, while research and intelligence tasks run at 6.41%, about 41% below the global 10.94%. Meanwhile the mechanics of use look entirely ordinary — 47.37% of conversations lean toward handing tasks to the tool versus 52.63% toward working alongside it, nearly identical to the global 48.62% split. There is no unusual caution or unusual delegation in how Benin uses these tools; the distinctiveness is entirely in what gets asked.
